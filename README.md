make.fun bounty entry: one original line roasting a smart fridge that
refuses to open after midnight, at most 200 characters. The line is the
whole entry — the fridge's official midnight apology note.

## The line

> It's 12:03 and my fridge just locked me out like a bouncer who checked my cholesterol and decided I wasn't on the list.

119 characters. A scene, not a quip: a 12:03 timestamp, the lockout at
the door, and the punchline as a bouncer who checked the owner's
cholesterol and decided he wasn't on the list. The target is the
fridge's own petty midnight rule — it reads like something a person
would actually say out loud at the door.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py  # writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (fallback /output)
```

`main.py` (stdlib only) validates the line is 1–200 characters on a single
line, writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`),
then reads it back to verify the written bytes match.
